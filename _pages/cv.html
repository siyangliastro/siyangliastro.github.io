Background

I received my Bachelor of Arts in Physics with high honors from the University of California, Berkeley. There, I built a foundation in instrumentation working with Professor George F. Smoot III on developing a silicon photomultiplier camera to search for optical counterparts to fast radio bursts and other astrophysical sub-millisecond transients. To work on this project and collaborate with various researchers, I visited Paris Diderot University in France, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in China, and Nazarbayev University in Kazakhstan. I also spent a summer working with Professor Shelley Wright and Dr. Jérôme Maire in the Pulsed All-sky Near-infrared Optical Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence group at the University of California, San Diego, to characterize a near-infrared discrete avalanche photodiode 5x5 array that is currently being used to search for extraterrestrial technosignatures and other near-infrared transients.
